Bingie Support

Need help with Bingie? We're here to help.

Bingie is a TV show rating and review app that lets you discover shows, rate what you've watched, write reviews, and connect with other viewers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Bingie uses Google Sign-In for account creation and authentication. Select the Google Sign-In option in the app and follow the prompts to sign in with your Google account.

How do I rate a TV show?

Search for a TV show in Bingie, open the show's page, and select your rating. Your rating will be saved to your Bingie account.

How do I write a review?

Open the page for the TV show you want to review and choose the option to write a review. Enter your review and submit it.

Reviews may be checked by automated moderation systems to help prevent inappropriate, harmful, abusive, or spam content.

Why was my review blocked or removed?

Bingie uses automated filtering to help identify content that may violate our community standards. Reviews containing inappropriate, abusive, harmful, offensive, or spam content may be blocked, restricted, or removed.

If you believe your review was incorrectly restricted, contact Bingie Support using the email address below.

How do I follow another user?

Visit another user's Bingie profile and select the Follow option. Their public activity may then appear in features available within Bingie.

How do I manage notifications?

You can manage Bingie's push notifications through your iPhone or iPad settings.

Go to:

Settings → Notifications → Bingie

From there, you can enable or disable notifications and adjust notification preferences.

How do I delete my Bingie account?

You can delete your Bingie account from within the app using the account deletion option in your profile or account settings.

Deleting your Bingie account does not delete your Google account.

If you have trouble deleting your account, contact Bingie Support using the email address below.

I can't sign in. What should I do?

If you're having trouble signing in with Google, try the following:

  • Make sure your device has an active internet connection.
  • Close and reopen Bingie.
  • Make sure you are signed into the correct Google account.
  • Restart your device and try again.

If the issue continues, contact Bingie Support.

I'm having trouble finding a TV show.

Try searching using the show's full title or a shorter version of the title.

Bingie uses third-party television data services to provide show information, so some newly released or less common shows may not immediately appear.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you encounter inappropriate, abusive, harmful, or otherwise unacceptable content in Bingie, please use any available reporting option within the app.

You may also contact Bingie Support and provide information about the content you are reporting.

Contact Bingie Support

If your question is not answered above, contact us and include as much information as possible about the issue.

Helpful details may include:

  • A description of what happened
  • The TV show or feature involved
  • Any error message you received
  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • A screenshot of the issue, if applicable
